WoodFit Puzzle Privacy Policy

WoodFit Puzzle is designed for private, offline play. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

No personal dataNothing is collected by the game.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how WoodFit Puzzle handles information. The game is a single-player Windows puzzle game published by KeyQuest Studio and distributed through the Microsoft Store.

2. Information We Collect

WoodFit Puzzle does not collect personal information, device identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data, usage analytics, crash analytics, or advertising identifiers.

3. Offline Operation and Local Data

The game works offline and does not require network access. Game preferences or progress may be stored locally on your Windows device so the game can remember your settings. This local information is not transmitted to KeyQuest Studio or 1ClickSoft.

4. Accounts, Advertising, and Purchases

WoodFit Puzzle does not provide user accounts, display advertising, use advertising networks, or offer in-app purchases.

5. Sharing and Selling Information

Because the game does not collect personal information, KeyQuest Studio does not sell, rent, disclose, or share personal information with third parties.

6. Microsoft and Windows Services

Microsoft Store and Windows may process information independently when you download, install, update, back up, or use platform features. Any such processing is governed by Microsoft's own terms and privacy statements and is not controlled by WoodFit Puzzle.

7. Children's Privacy

WoodFit Puzzle does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Security

WoodFit Puzzle does not transmit personal data to KeyQuest Studio and does not maintain a remote database of player information.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent through the 1ClickSoft contact page. Please mention “WoodFit Puzzle” in your message.
